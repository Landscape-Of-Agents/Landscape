# The Landscape overview

Open **Landscape** in the activity bar.

- **Overview** shows Crane's status, how many selections pass, fail or are unresolved, and one-click actions. Every selection is listed, and you can search and filter the list. Click one to reveal it in the editor. The overview also lists zones and flows, the most serious findings, and what you did in this window.
- **Governance** shows policies with their selections, zones, flows and context packs, with their status.

The status bar shows the same counts. Hover over it for a summary and links.
